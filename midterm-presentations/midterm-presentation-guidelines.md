# Midterm Presentation Guidelines

### Thursday October 8

## Overview

The goal of this project is for students to see both the core and extended material presented in class. Also, to give students an applied look at solving data analysis problems using these methods in R. With this goal in mind, the presentation will consist of:

- Find your own dataset
- Model and forecast
  - Use core material presented during lecture
  - Use extended material from textbooks or found from other resources
- Create slides using Quarto
- Prepare a 20 minute presentation

## Presentation guidelines

Each presentation should be a "mini lecture" on the analysis. The presentations will be evaluated on the ability to show the following:

1. **Dataset**
   - Find your own data that does not already have an existing data analysis
   - It should be a multivariate dataset. Either to be jointly modeled or a target series with covariates.
   - Do an empirical data analysis of your series

2. **Modeling**
   - Introduce notation and relevant mathematical background for multiple models we have learned and models that are extensions to what we have learned.
   - Describe the theory behind your model choices
   - Fit model to your dataset
   - Discuss the model fit diagnostics

3. **Forecasting**
   - Use fit model to forecast your time series
   - Compare forecasts using cross validation for multiple time horizons
   - Discuss the results and compare the two models
   - Discuss practical implications of these forecasts in context of the data

4. **Slides and Presentation**
   - Produce slides of results and methods using Quarto
   - Be sure your code and output is formatted to look nice on the screen
   - Upload a link to your slides to GitHub.
   - Students will be randomly selected for presentation order starting.
